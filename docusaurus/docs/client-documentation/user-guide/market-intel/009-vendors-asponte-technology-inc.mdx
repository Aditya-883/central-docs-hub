Explore, filter, and export comprehensive profiles of government contractors and vendors using the Market Intel module. Access this page by navigating to **Market Intel → Vendors**.

![Vendors Page](/img/client/009-vendors-asponte-technology-inc-screenshot.png)

## Search and filter vendors

Use the search bar and filter tools to narrow down the vendor list by recipient name, business type codes, parent legal business name, or city.

1. Click into the **Type Recipient Name, Business Type Codes, Parent Legal Business Name, City** search field.
2. Select an operator from the dropdown menu (such as **Contains**, **Starting**, **Ending**, or **Exact match**).
3. Type your search criteria and click **Apply** to update the table results.
4. Click **Filter** to access advanced criteria, add clauses, or apply saved filters.

## Vendor table reference

The vendor data table displays detailed information across multiple columns. 

| Column | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Recipient** | Organization or entity receiving the award or contract. |
| **Parent Recipient** | Legal name of the recipient’s parent organization or company. |
| **Business Type** | Socioeconomic and business classifications for the vendor. |
| **Top 5 Prime Customer** | Primary government customers associated with the vendor. |

## Export vendor records

You can export vendor data sets for offline analysis or reporting.

1. Click the **Export** button located above the right side of the data table.
2. Configure your export range using the **Start** and **End** fields.
3. Confirm your selection to download the records.
