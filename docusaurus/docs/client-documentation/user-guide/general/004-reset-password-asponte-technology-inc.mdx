Update your account credentials by entering your current password and providing a new, secure password that meets all complexity requirements.

![Change Password]( /img/client/004-reset-password-asponte-technology-inc-screenshot.png )

## Change Password Form

Use the form to update your security credentials. Your new password must be different from any previously used passwords and must satisfy the following complexity rules:
- Must be at least 8 characters
- Must contain an upper case letter
- Must contain a lower case letter
- Must contain a number
- Must contain a special character

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Current Password** | Password | Yes | Enter your existing active password. Click the eye icon to toggle visibility. |
| **New Password** | Password | Yes | Enter your desired new password. Click the eye icon to toggle visibility. |
| **Reenter Password** | Password | Yes | Re-enter your new password to confirm. Click the eye icon to toggle visibility. |

## Actions

1. Complete the required fields in the **Change Password** form.
2. Verify that all password requirement checkmarks are met.
3. Click **Change Password** to save your new credentials, or click **Cancel** to discard your changes and return to your [Dashboard](./dashboard.mdx).
