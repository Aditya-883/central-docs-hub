View your centralized operational overview, lead metrics, and market intelligence signals by navigating to **Dashboard**. After you sign in, you land on your main [Dashboard](./dashboard.mdx) view.

![Dashboard Overview](/img/client/001-dashboard-asponte-technology-inc-screenshot.png)

## Header and navigation elements

The top navigation bar allows you to switch between primary GovCon360 modules. 

| Navigation Tab | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Dashboard** | Returns you to the main operational overview. |
| **Market Intel** | Accesses market intelligence data and solicitation signals. |
| **Lead Prospector** | Opens your active pursuit leads and pipeline management tools. |
| **Business Development** | Manages business development pipelines and opportunities. |
| **Teaming Partnerships** | Explores potential teaming and partnership opportunities. |
| **Proposals** | Manages proposal development and submissions. |
| **Contracts** | Tracks awarded contracts and administration. |
| **Administration** | Accesses system settings and user configurations. |

## Your company profile

The top-left panel displays core identifier details for your organization, including your **Cage Code**, **UEI**, and **Primary NAICS** code. 

| Element | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **View GovCon360 Profile** | Opens a detailed view of your organization's GovCon360 profile. |
| **Manage Profile** | Allows you to update your corporate information and settings. |
| **Complete Company Profile** | Guides you through the remaining steps to get your business fully GovCon-ready. |

## Lead Prospector and Market Intel

The middle and bottom sections of the dashboard provide immediate visibility into your pipeline health and active market signals.

- **Lead Prospector**: Summarizes your pipeline metrics, including **New Leads**, **Active Leads**, **Expiring** leads, and **SAM.gov** solicitations added recently.
- **Market Intel**: Highlights actionable signals such as **Active Buying Agencies**, **Contracts ending soon**, **New Solicitations**, and key **Agency Contacts**.
