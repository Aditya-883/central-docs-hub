Use the sign-in page to authenticate and access your [Dashboard](./dashboard.mdx). 

![Sign In](/img/client/login-page-screenshot.png)

## Sign in to your account

1. Enter your credentials in the authentication form on the right side of the screen.
2. Click **Sign In** to access your workspace.

| Element | Type | Required | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Email Address** | Text | No | Enter the email address associated with your account. |
| **Password** | Password | No | Enter your account password. Click the eye icon to toggle password visibility. |
| **Remember me** | Checkbox | No | Keep your session active on this device. |
| **Forgot Password?** | Link | No | Reset your password if you cannot remember it. |
