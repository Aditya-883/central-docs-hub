## Overview

Market Intelligence transforms complex historical government spending data and agency structures into a clear roadmap for your business. It helps you determine who needs your product, how they prefer to buy it, and where you should focus your time and resources to succeed in the federal market. You can explore how [Business Developers Track what matters.](./solutions-business-developers.mdx) to enhance your strategy.

## Page Features

### Federal Agency & Vendor Entities
Map out the exact organizations operating in your space to build targeted relationships:
- **Vendors**: Explore businesses winning contracts in your field to identify potential established prime contractors looking for specialized partners.
- **Agencies**: Bypass noise and identify specific departments with a history of purchasing your type of product.
- **Offices**: Drill down past main agencies to discover specific regional offices and facilities making actual buying decisions.

### Government Spend Insights
View past and present purchasing habits without overwhelming spreadsheets:
- **Awards**: View previous contract winners and final dollar amounts to establish a realistic baseline for pricing your own solutions.
- **Solicitations**: Analyze past solicitations to spot seasonal buying trends and cycles, helping you anticipate when agencies prepare to purchase.
- **Contracts**: Dive into active contract scopes and expiration dates to build long-term strategies and prepare for future re-competes.
- **Vehicles**: Understand preferred agency purchasing methods and checkout lanes to position your business for seamless sales.

### Taking Action on Your Research
Save search filters and share them directly with your team. Push insights directly into your workflow:
- Send potential leads straight to your Lead Workspace.
- Move key Agencies and Vendors directly into your Accounts.
- Add crucial connection makers right into your Contacts.

### Frequently Asked Questions
- **How do I find out which government agencies actually buy my specific product?** Use the Agencies search to filter by your specific product type and view departments and local offices with a history of purchasing it.
- **Can I see how much the government usually pays for solutions like mine?** Yes, the Awards search details previous contract winners and final dollar amounts to establish competitive pricing baselines.
- **How do I know when an existing contract is going to expire so I can prepare to bid?** Use the Contracts search to view current scopes and expiration dates, spotting upcoming re-competes 12 to 18 months in advance.
- **I am a small business. How can researching other vendors help me?** Identify established prime contractors via the Vendors search who frequently win large bids and need capable, specialized small businesses to team up with as subcontractors.
- **What are contract vehicles and do I need one to win a bid?** Use the Vehicles search to identify preferred purchasing pathways used by target agencies so you can position your business or partner with someone holding that vehicle.
