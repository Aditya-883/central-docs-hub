## Overview

The Market Intelligence page provides a comprehensive overview of how GovCon 360 helps businesses navigate the federal marketplace. It translates complex historical government spend and agency structures into clear roadmaps, enabling users to identify who is buying specific solutions, analyze purchasing trends, and determine where to focus time and resources. 

Users can explore this feature to understand federal agency and vendor entities, track purchasing trends, and connect insights directly to their sales pipeline. For more information on business development strategies, track what matters with **[Business Developers Track what matters.](./solutions-business-developers.mdx)** or review the **[Pricing](./pricing.mdx)** and **[Login](./home-govcon-360-public-site-liferay.mdx)** pages. You can also return to the **[Home](./home.mdx)** page at any time.

## Page Features

### Federal Agency & Vendor Entities
The Entities tool helps map out organizations operating in your space to build targeted relationships.
- **Vendors**: Explore businesses winning contracts in your field to identify established prime contractors who may be looking for specialized, capable partners.
- **Agencies**: Identify specific agencies with a history of buying your type of product to focus outreach efforts.
- **Offices**: Drill down past main agencies to find specific regional offices and facilities making actual buying decisions.

### Government Spend Insights
View past and present purchasing habits without being overwhelmed by spreadsheets.
- **Awards**: View past awards, previous winning contractors, and final dollar amounts to establish a realistic pricing baseline.
- **Solicitations**: Analyze past solicitations to spot seasonal buying trends and cycles, anticipating when agencies prepare to purchase.
- **Contracts**: Review active contract scopes and expiration dates to build long-term strategies and prepare for future re-competes.
- **Vehicles**: Understand preferred agency purchasing methods and checkout lanes to position your business for seamless sales.

### Taking Action on Your Research
Market Intelligence actively feeds your pipeline by allowing you to save search filters and share them with your team. Findings can be pushed directly into your workflow:
- Send potential leads straight to your Lead Workspace.
- Move key Agencies and Vendors directly into your Accounts.
- Add crucial connection makers right into your Contacts.

### Frequently Asked Questions
- **How do I find out which government agencies actually buy my specific product?** Use the Agencies search to filter by product type and view departments and local offices with a purchasing history.
- **Can I see how much the government usually pays for solutions like mine?** Yes, the Awards search details previous contract winners and final dollar amounts for realistic pricing baselines.
- **How do I know when an existing contract is going to expire so I can prepare to bid?** Use the Contracts search to view expiration dates and spot upcoming re-competes 12 to 18 months in advance.
- **I am a small business. How can researching other vendors help me?** Use the Vendors search to identify established prime contractors who may need capable small businesses as subcontractors.
- **What are contract vehicles and do I need one to win a bid?** Contract vehicles are established purchasing pathways; the Vehicles search helps you see which paths your target agencies prefer.

:::tip
Use the saved search filters in Market Intelligence to instantly align your team and push qualified data directly into your accounts, contacts, and lead workspaces.
