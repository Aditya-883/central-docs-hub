## Overview

The GovCon 360 public home page serves as the entry point to the government contracting software platform. It demystifies the federal marketplace for small businesses, helping users find contract opportunities, analyze market intelligence, and manage business development pipelines. Users can explore core features, review platform capabilities, and access pathways for **[Business Developers Track what matters.](./solutions-business-developers.mdx)**, **[Market Intelligence Know what's coming.](./features-market-intelligence.mdx)**, subscription **[Pricing](./pricing.mdx)**, and account **[Login](./home-govcon-360-public-site-liferay.mdx)**.

## Page Features

### Government Contracting Software, Simplified

At GovCon360, the platform simplifies federal contracting for small businesses, whether looking for a first contract or identifying buying agencies.

### Core Features: Government Contracting Tools to Compete and Win

*   **Market Intelligence**: Know Your Market. Stop guessing where your next contract will come from by viewing entity profiles and office spending habits to identify agency trends and targets.
*   **Lead Prospector**: Capture the Right Leads. Access a database of millions of contracts and thousands of active agencies to filter through noise and focus on high-probability wins.
*   **Business Development**: Organize Your Way to a Win. Manage every step of the bidding process with centralized pipelines, tracked opportunities, and organized contacts and tasks.
*   **Teaming Partnerships**: Find the right fit, faster. Connect with vetted government contract teaming partners, align strengths, manage joint bids, and collaborate in a secure workspace.

### One Platform, Powerful Data

Consolidates data from over 40+ sources including GSA, Symphony, SBA.gov, USASpending.gov, and SAM.gov into a single unified platform.

### Who It's For

*   **Small & Micro Businesses**: Provides a clear path from first steps to contract readiness with tools, training, and community support.
*   **[Business Developers Track what matters.](./solutions-business-developers.mdx)**: Offers an intelligent dashboard to track and prioritize active leads and connect with teaming partners.
*   **Resellers**: Helps access real-time buyer demand, match with partners, and build standout profiles to increase agency visibility.
*   **Finding Teaming Partners**: Fills capability gaps, discovers socio-economic certifications, and manages joint federal bids.

### Built with Security in Mind

The SaaS platform is fully secure and compliant with industry standards, protecting data and privacy from log-in to data export.
