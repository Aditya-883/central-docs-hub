## Overview

GovCon 360 demystifies the federal marketplace for small businesses. Whether you are looking for your very first contract or trying to figure out which agencies buy your services, our government contracting software gives you the simple, powerful tools to find and win government work with total confidence.

## Page Features

- **Government Contracting Software, Simplified**: We took the most complicated parts of federal contracting and turned them into straightforward tools so your small team can find the right opportunities and manage bids without feeling overwhelmed.
- **Government Contracting Tools to Compete and Win**:
  - **[Market Intelligence](./features-market-intelligence.mdx)**: Know Your Market. Stop guessing where your next contract will come from by seeing the full picture of the federal marketplace. We provide detailed data on entity profiles and office spending habits so you can identify agency trends and agency targets.
  - **Lead Prospector**: Capture the Right Leads. Access a database of millions of contracts and thousands of active agencies to find the opportunities that fit your business, allowing you to filter through noise and focus on high-probability wins.
  - **Business Development**: Organize Your Way to a Win. Manage every step of your bidding process, track opportunities, and manage your accounts in one centralized workspace.
  - **Teaming Partnerships**: Find the right fit, faster. Connect with vetted government contract teaming partners to build winning alliances, align strengths, and manage joint bids in a secure workspace.
- **One Platform, Powerful Data**: Integrates essential information from over 40+ data sources, including GSA, Symphony, SBA.gov, USASpending.gov, and SAM.gov.
- **Who It's For**:
  - **Small & Micro Businesses**: Provides a clear and supportive path from your first step to contract readiness, offering tools, training, and community.
  - **[Business Developers](./solutions-business-developers.mdx)**: Offers an intelligent dashboard to clearly track and prioritize opportunities, access active leads, and connect instantly with qualified teaming partners.
  - **Resellers**: Helps access real-time buyer demand, match with right partners, and build standout profiles to increase agency visibility.
  - **Finding Teaming Partnerships**: Helps fill capability gaps, find precise socio-economic certifications, and manage joint federal bids.
- **Built with Security in Mind**: Fully secure SaaS platform compliant with industry standards to protect your data and private insights.
- **[Pricing](./pricing.mdx)**: Access smarter tools with a premium account to help you win more contracts and strategically grow.
