## Overview

GovCon 360 demystifies the federal marketplace for small businesses. Whether you are looking for your very first contract or trying to determine which agencies buy your services, the platform provides simple, powerful tools to find and win government work with confidence. You can also review plans and details on the **[Pricing](./pricing.mdx)** page.

## Page Features

- **Government Contracting Software, Simplified**: Straightforward tools designed to help small teams find the right opportunities and manage bids without feeling overwhelmed. Learn more about navigating the platform at **[home](./home.mdx)**.
- **Market Intelligence**: Stop guessing where your next contract will come from. View detailed data on entity profiles and office spending habits to identify agency trends and targets, and track **[Market Intelligence Know what's coming.](./features-market-intelligence.mdx)**.
- **Lead Prospector**: Access a database of millions of contracts and thousands of active agencies to filter through noise and focus on high-probability wins.
- **Business Development**: Manage every step of your bidding process, track opportunities, organize contacts, and manage accounts in one centralized workspace. Explore options for **[Business Developers Track what matters.](./solutions-business-developers.mdx)**.
- **Teaming Partnerships**: Find vetted government contract teaming partners, align strengths, manage joint bids, and foster mutual trust in a secure, shared workspace.
- **One Platform, Powerful Data**: Integrates data from over 40+ sources—including GSA, Symphony, SBA.gov, USASpending.gov, and SAM.gov—into a single platform.
- **Who It's For**: Tailored capabilities for Small & Micro Businesses, Business Developers, Resellers, and teams seeking Teaming Partners.
- **Built with Security in Mind**: Fully secure SaaS platform compliant with industry standards to protect your data and private insights.
