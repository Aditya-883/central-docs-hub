## Overview

The GovCon 360 platform home page serves as the primary entry point to demystify the federal marketplace for small businesses. Users can explore core features like **[Market Intelligence](./features-market-intelligence.mdx)**, learn about target solutions including **[Business Developers Track what matters.](./solutions-business-developers.mdx)**, review **[Pricing](./pricing.mdx)** options, and access the central public portal at **[https://www.nlproducts.net/home](./home.mdx)**.

## Page Features

- **Government Contracting Software, Simplified**: Demystifies the federal marketplace for small businesses seeking their first contract or evaluating agency buyers.
- **Government Contracting Tools to Compete and Win**:
  - **Market Intelligence**: Provides detailed data on entity profiles and office spending habits to identify agency trends and targets.
  - **Lead Prospector**: Accesses a database of millions of contracts and active agencies with filtering tools for high-probability wins.
  - **Business Development**: Centralized workspace to track opportunities, manage accounts, organize contacts, and handle tasks.
  - **Teaming Partnerships**: Secure, shared workspace to find vetted teaming partners, align strengths, and manage joint bids.
- **One Platform, Powerful Data**: Consolidates information from over 40+ data sources including GSA, Symphony, SBA.gov, USASpending.gov, and SAM.gov.
- **Who It's For**: Tailored pathways and tools designed for Small & Micro Businesses, **[Business Developers Track what matters.](./solutions-business-developers.mdx)**, Resellers, and Teaming Partnerships.
- **Built with Security in Mind**: Fully secure SaaS platform compliant with industry standards to protect data and privacy.

## Step-by-Step Workflow

1. Navigate to the public site home page and review the available solutions and features.
2. Select **Customize**, **Reject all**, or **Accept all** on the cookie banner to manage cookie preferences.
3. Click **Schedule a Demo** to request a platform tour and explore premium account capabilities.

## Form Fields Reference

| Field Label | Type | Required | Validation / Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cookie Preferences | button / dialog | No | Manage tracking and personalized content preferences via **Customize**, **Reject all**, or **Accept all**. |
