## Overview

The Pricing page provides transparent, capacity-based pricing information built specifically for government contractors. It outlines available plan packages, tier comparisons, and feature breakdowns to help users select the right option for their business needs. You can review plan tiers, compare capacity limits, and access platform features on [Home](./home.mdx).

## Page Features

- **Billing Frequency Options**: Switch between monthly billing and annual billing (with savings up to 20%).
- **Plan Packages**
  - **Small Business Tier**: Capacity-capped for focused pipelines. Tailored for small businesses, solopreneurs, 8(a), HUBZone, and growing teams. Includes AI-generated public-facing searches, custom stages & tags, built-in PWin scoring, and account & contact management.
  - **Premium Tier**: Unlimited access to the full suite of professional tools. Tailored for established firms, capture managers, and high-volume teams. Includes unlimited capacity (uncapped active leads, deals, and searches), unlimited exports (uncapped CSV/PDF exports & historical depth), shared searches for team collaboration, and limitless networking (unlimited partner match inquiries).
- **Compare Tiers**: Line-by-line breakdown comparing the Small Business Tier versus the unlimited Premium Tier and usage-based pricing.
- **Full Feature Breakdown**: Complete suite of features and resources available to users. You can explore additional capabilities such as **[Market Intelligence Know what's coming.](./features-market-intelligence.mdx)** and track what matters using **[Business Developers Track what matters.](./solutions-business-developers.mdx)**.

## Additional Information

If you are an existing user looking to access your account, you can use the **[Login](./home-govcon-360-public-site-liferay.mdx)** option from the site navigation.
