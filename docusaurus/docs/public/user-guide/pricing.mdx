## Overview

The GovCon360 Pricing page outlines the platform's transparent, usage-based pricing model designed to help government contractors manage software costs without hidden fees or prohibitive feature gating. Users can review the platform's pricing philosophy, examine industry pain points addressed by GovCon360, and compare subscription tiers tailored to different workload capacities and business growth stages. From this page, users can explore [Market Intelligence Track what's coming.](./features-market-intelligence.mdx) capabilities and [Business Developers Track what matters.](./solutions-business-developers.mdx) solutions, or navigate to [Login](./home-govcon-360-public-site-liferay.mdx) to access their accounts. ## Page Features

- **GovCon360 Pricing Philosophy**: Built by a small, minority-owned business to simplify the GovCon lifecycle and remove complex, confusing pricing models that hold contractors back.
- **The 5 Broken Rules of GovCon Software Pricing**: Addresses major industry roadblocks, including:
  - Opaque, call-for-quote pricing that makes Total Cost of Ownership projection nearly impossible.
  - Feature gating that locks growing businesses out of top-tier AI and intelligence tools.
  - The shelfware problem caused by cyclical federal contracting periods.
  - The two extremes trap between bloated all-in-one enterprise suites and disjointed niche add-on tools.
  - Excessive complexity that leads to delayed purchasing decisions.
- **The Solution: Pricing Built for Your Actual Workload**: Features a per-seat, usage-based capacity model where every user gets access to premium features from Day 1, with seat costs dictated strictly by workload volume.
- **Subscription Tiers**:
  - **Tier 1: The Small Business Tier**: Designed for solopreneurs and small teams managing a focused, low-volume pipeline under the "Rule of 10" capacity limits.
  - **Tier 2: The Premium Bundle**: Built for established firms, enterprise capture managers, and fast-growing teams running high-volume bidding operations with unlimited data, historical depths, and exports.
- **Total Transparency on Limits**: Outlines specific capacity caps for Small Business Tier users, such as active saved searches, CSV/PDF exports, lead workspace capacity, and teaming partner collaboration limits.

## Additional Information

When users on the Small Business tier reach their capacity limits (such as adding an 11th active opportunity), it serves as a milestone indicating business growth. Users can clean up older deals to make room or choose to seamlessly upgrade to the Premium Bundle.

:::tip
GovCon360's transparent pricing ensures you only pay for the capacity you consume, allowing you to focus on winning federal contracts rather than worrying about software bills.
:::
