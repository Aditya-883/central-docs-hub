Manage your organization's legal identifiers, entity types, and registration details via **Accounts → Customer Content → Company Details**.

![page](/img/admin/005-accounts-govcon-360-internal-liferay-screenshot.png)

## Company Details

Use the setup wizard to review and update your organization's core profile attributes across four sequential steps: **General Information**, **Point of Contact**, **Business Overview**, and **Company Details**.

### Entity Identifiers

Complete the required and optional fields to maintain accurate corporate registration records:

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Name** | Text | Yes | Enter the official legal business name. |
| **Legal Structure / Entity Type** | Dropdown | No | Select your corporate entity structure (e.g., 2L). |
| **DUNS** | Text | No | Enter your Data Universal Numbering System identifier. |
| **Primary NAICS** | Dropdown | No | Select or search for your primary North American Industry Classification System code. |
| **CAGE Code** | Text | No | Enter your Commercial and Government Entity code. |
| **SIC** | Dropdown | No | Select or search for your Standard Industrial Classification code. |

### Managing Wizard Steps

1. Review or modify information in the **Company Details** section.
2. Click **Previous** to return to the **Business Overview** step if you need to make changes to prior sections.
3. Click **Save** to record your updates and advance through the configuration wizard.
