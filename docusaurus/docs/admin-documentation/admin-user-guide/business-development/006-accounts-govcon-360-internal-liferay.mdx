Manage your organizational entity identifiers and corporate profile information under **Accounts → Accounts → Company Details**.

![page](/img/admin/006-accounts-govcon-360-internal-liferay-screenshot.png)

## Company Details

Use the multistep progress tracker at the top to navigate between **General Information**, **Point of Contact**, **Business Overview**, and **Company Details**. 

### Entity Identifiers

Complete the organizational profile fields to maintain accurate registration and classification records.

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Name** | Text | Yes | Enter the official legal name of your organization. |
| **Legal Structure / Entity Type** | Dropdown | No | Select your corporate structure or entity type. |
| **DUNS** | Text | No | Enter your Data Universal Numbering System identifier. |
| **Primary NAICS** | Dropdown | No | Enter or select your primary North American Industry Classification System code. |
| **CAGE Code** | Text | No | Enter your Commercial and Government Entity code. |
| **SIC** | Dropdown | No | Enter or select your Standard Industrial Classification code. |

## Navigation Actions

Use the action buttons at the bottom of the form to move through the setup workflow:

- Click **Previous** to return to the previous section of the setup wizard.
- Click **Save** to store your current changes.
