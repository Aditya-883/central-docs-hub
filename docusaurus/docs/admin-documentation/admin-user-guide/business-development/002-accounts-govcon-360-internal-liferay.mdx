Use the Accounts page to configure your core organization profile, update identifiers, and set up your portal branding. Navigate to this section by selecting **Accounts** from the primary navigation bar.

## General Information

The **General Information** step guides you through setting up your organization's foundational details. A progress indicator at the top tracks your path through **General Information**, **Point of Contact**, **Business Overview**, and **Company Details**.

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UEI** | Text | Yes | Enter your organization Unique Entity Identifier. |
| **Account Type** | Toggle | Yes | Select between **B2C** and **B2B**. |
| **Enterprise** | Toggle | No | Select **NO** or **YES** to indicate enterprise scale. |
| **Organization Name** | Text | Yes | Enter your full legal organization name. |
| **Sub Domain** | Text | Yes | Define your custom subdomain preceding `.nlproducts.net`. |
| **Logo** | Upload | No | Upload a company logo to increase profile visibility with potential teaming partners using **Upload Logo**. |
