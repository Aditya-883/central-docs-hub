Manage the primary contact details for your organization by navigating to **Accounts → Point of Contact**. This step is part of the multi-step account configuration wizard, sitting between **General Information** and **Business Overview**.

![Point of Contact](/img/admin/003-accounts-govcon-360-internal-liferay-screenshot.png)

## Point of Contact

Use the form fields to enter or update the representative's contact information. 

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First Name** | Text | Yes | The first name of the contact. |
| **Last Name** | Text | Yes | The last name of the contact. |
| **Email** | Email | Yes | The professional email address of the contact. |
| **Designation** | Text | No | Enter the title or role of the contact. |
| **Office Number** | Phone / Select | No | Select your country code from the dropdown and enter the phone number. |
| **Extension** | Text | No | Enter any relevant office phone extension. |

## Navigation and Actions

Use the progress steps at the top of the panel (**General Information**, **Point of Contact**, **Business Overview**, **Company Details**) to jump directly to other sections, or use the buttons at the bottom of the form:

1. Click **Previous** to return to the **General Information** step.
2. Click **Next** to save your changes and proceed to the **Business Overview** step.
