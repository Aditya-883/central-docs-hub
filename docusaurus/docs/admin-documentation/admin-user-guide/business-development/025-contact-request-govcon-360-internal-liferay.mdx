Submit a contact request to the support or internal team by filling out the required contact details. You can access this page through the application navigation.

![Contact Request](/img/admin/025-contact-request-govcon-360-internal-liferay-screenshot.png)

## General Information

Use the **GENERAL** section to provide your personal and professional details along with your inquiry.

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First Name** | Text | No | Enter your given name. |
| **Last Name** | Text | No | Enter your family name. |
| **Company Name** | Text | No | Enter your organization or company name. |
| **Email Address** | Text | No | Enter a valid email address for correspondence. |
| **Phone Number** | Text | No | Enter your contact phone number. |
| **Message** | Textarea | No | Enter your inquiry or feedback (up to 1000 characters). |
