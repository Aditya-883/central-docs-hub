Manage client-specific market intelligence, data feeds, and lead prospecting tools by accessing **Customer Content** from the main navigation bar.

![Customer Content](/img/admin/008-data-management-screenshot.png)

## Choose the client you want to work with

1. Click the **Choose the client you want to work with** dropdown menu located near the top of the content area.
2. Select your desired client organization to load their customized dashboard content, market intelligence, and prospecting workspaces.

## Market Intel and Lead Prospector

The main workspace divides client data into two core sections:

| Section | Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Market Intel** | Manage market intelligence content and insights, including **Spend Insights** (Awards, Solicitations, Contracts, Vehicles) and **Entities** (Vendors, Agencies, Offices, People). |
| **Lead Prospector** | Manage prospecting content and lead opportunities through the **Lead Workspace**, **Find & Nurture**, **Procurement Notices**, **SAM.GOV Leads**, and **Re-Competes**. |

## Header elements and submenus

The top navigation bar provides access to broader system areas including **Accounts**, **Data Management**, **Commerce**, **Marketing Management**, and **Administration**. Hovering over **Customer Content** opens a quick-access dropdown menu for data administration tasks such as the **Ingestion Schedule**, **Schedule Summary**, **Ingestion Dashabord**, **Parser Emails**, and **Grid Management**.
