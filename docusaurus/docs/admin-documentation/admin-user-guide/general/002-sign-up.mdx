Create a new GovCon360 account by providing your name and email to get started. Navigate to the sign-up page to begin your registration.

![Sign up](/img/admin/002-sign-up-screenshot.png)

## Create an account

Use the sign-up form to set up your profile and access the platform. If you already have an account, click the **Log in** link to access your dashboard.

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **First name** | text | No | Enter your first name. |
| **Last name** | text | No | Enter your last name. |
| **Email Address** | email | No | Enter your professional email address. |

1. Enter your details in the **First name**, **Last name**, and **Email Address** fields.
2. Click **Get Started** to submit your information and create your account.
3. Alternatively, click **NL Identity** to authenticate using single sign-on.
