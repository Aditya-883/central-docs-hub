The Customer Content page allows you to select a client and access their specific market intelligence and lead prospecting modules. You can reach this page by selecting **Customer Content** in the top navigation bar.

![Customer Content page](/img/admin/007-customer-content-govcon-360-internal-liferay-screenshot.png)

## Select a client

1. Locate the **Choose the client you want to work with** selection field near the top of the main panel.
2. Click the field and select the desired client from the dropdown menu to load their specific workspace cards.

## Market Intel and Lead Prospector

Once a client is selected, the main panel displays two primary management sections:

| Section | Description & Features |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Market Intel** | Manage market intelligence content and insights. Includes **Spend Insights** (**Awards**, **Solicitations**, **Contracts**, **Vehicles**) and **Entities** (**Vendors**, **Agencies**, **Offices**, **People**). |
| **Lead Prospector** | Manage prospecting content and lead opportunities. Includes links to the **Lead Workspace**, **Find & Nurture**, **Procurement Notices**, **SAM.GOV Leads**, and **Re-Competes**. |
