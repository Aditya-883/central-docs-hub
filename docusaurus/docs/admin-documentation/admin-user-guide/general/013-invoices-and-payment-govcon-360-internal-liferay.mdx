Manage invoices, track payment status, and organize billing information for B2B customers under **Commerce** → **Invoices and Payment**.

![Invoices and Payment](/img/admin/013-invoices-and-payment-govcon-360-internal-liferay-screenshot.png)

## Invoices and payment overview

The Invoices and Payment page provides a summary of your billing metrics, including **Outstanding** balances and **Draft** invoices. You can search, filter, and upload new invoices as needed.

## Managing invoices

Use the search bar and filter dropdowns to find specific customer invoices.

| Element | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Search by recipient name or invoice numnber** | Text | No | Enter a keyword to locate a specific invoice or customer. |
| **All Status** | Dropdown | No | Filter invoices by their current status. |
| **All Payment Method** | Dropdown | No | Filter invoices by payment method (e.g., **ACH**). |

## Uploading an invoice

1. Click **Upload Invoice** in the upper-right corner of the main panel.
2. In the **Select R...** modal, pick the **Recipient name** to automatically populate payment terms and details.
3. Click **Continue** to proceed or **Cancel** to abort the upload.
