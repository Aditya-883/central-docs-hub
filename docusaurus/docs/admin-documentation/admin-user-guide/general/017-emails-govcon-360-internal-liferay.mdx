description: "Compose and send emails directly from the platform using the built-in email editor."

Use the Compose Email page to send messages to recipients. Access this page by navigating through the application modules to the email management interface.

![Compose Email](/img/admin/017-emails-govcon-360-internal-liferay-screenshot.png)

## Compose Email form fields

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **From** | Text | No | Displays the sender email address or portal admin identifier. |
| **Enter recipient email addresses** | Text | No | Enter the primary destination addresses for the email. You can also click **CC** or **BCC** to include additional recipients. |
| **Enter a subject** | Text | No | Provide a short descriptive subject line for your message. |
| **Message** | Textarea | No | The main body of the email. Use the built-in formatting toolbar to style your text. |

## Formatting and actions toolbar

The message editor features a rich text toolbar allowing you to customize your correspondence:

- **Text Color** and **Background Color**: Adjust the text and highlight colors.
- **Bold (Ctrl+B)**, **Italic (Ctrl+I)**, **Underline (Ctrl+U)**, and **Strikethrough**: Apply standard text styles.
- **Size**: Select font sizes for your message.
- **Align Left**, **Center**, and **Align Right**: Control paragraph alignment.
- **Insert/Remove Numbered List** and **Insert/Remove Bulleted List**: Organize content into lists.
- **Attach**: Add files to your outgoing message.
- **Cancel**: Discard your draft.
- **Send Email**: Transmit the completed message to your recipients.
