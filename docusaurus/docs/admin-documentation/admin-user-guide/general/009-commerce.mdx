Manage client-specific contracts, invoices, market intelligence, and lead generation tools from the **Customer Content** page.

## Choose the client you want to work with

1. Navigate to **Customer Content** to open the client workspace selection screen.
2. Click the **Choose the client you want to work with** dropdown field and select the desired company (such as **Asponte Technology Inc**).

Once a client is selected, the page displays two primary management sections:

| Section | Available Sub-Categories & Links | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Market Intel** | **Awards**, **Solicitations**, **Contracts**, **Vehicles**, **Vendors**, **Agencies**, **Offices**, **People** | Manage market intelligence content, spend insights, and entity relationships for the selected client. |
| **Lead Prospector** | **Lead Workspace**, **Find & Nurture**, **Procurement Notices**, **SAM.GOV Leads**, **Re-Competes** | Manage prospecting content and discover new lead opportunities for the selected client. |

## Navigation elements

The top navigation bar provides access to broader system modules including **Accounts**, **Customer Content**, **Data Management**, **Commerce**, **Marketing Management**, and **Administration**. Hovering over **Commerce** reveals dropdown options for **Customer Contract** and **Invoices and Payment**.
