Use the login page to authenticate and access your GovCon360 account. Navigate to the login screen when first launching the application.

![page](/img/admin/001-homepage-screenshot.png)

## Sign In

1. Enter your registered email address in the **Email Address** field.
2. Enter your password in the **Password** field.
3. Check the **Remember Me** box if you want your session remembered on that device.
4. Click **Sign In** to access the platform.

After you sign in, you land on your main dashboard to begin managing opportunities and tasks.

## Form Fields

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enter email** | Email | Yes | Enter the email address associated with your account. |
| **Password** | Password | Yes | Enter your account password. |
| **Remember Me** | Checkbox | No | Keeps you signed in on the current device. |
