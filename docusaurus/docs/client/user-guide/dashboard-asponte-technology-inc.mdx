## Overview

The Dashboard serves as the central command center for Asponte Technology Inc. It provides a comprehensive overview of your organization's GovCon readiness, active lead generation metrics, procurement notices, and critical market intelligence. You can quickly monitor key business development indicators, review expiring contracts, track active buying agencies, and navigate to detailed sections like **[User Profile](./users.mdx)**, **[Change Password](./reset-password.mdx)**, or **[View All](./notifications.mdx)** notifications.

## Page Features

- **Company Overview & Status**: Displays organizational details including website, primary contact, active SAM.gov status, and certifications (VOSB, SDVOSB).
- **GovCon-Ready Progress**: Tracks completion progress for your company profile portfolio and company banner.
- **Lead Prospector**: Summarizes active pursuits and procurement notices, tracking metrics across multiple channels:
  - **SAM.gov**: Solicitations added in the last 14 days.
  - **Ebuy RFx**: GSA eBuy procurement notices added in the last 14 days.
  - **Email Parsers**: Email procurement notices added in the last 14 days.
  - **Symphony RFx**: Symphony procurement notices added in the last 14 days.
  - **New Leads**: Leads added to In Evaluation in the last 7 days.
  - **Active Leads**: Leads currently in Plans to Pursue.
  - **Expiring**: Workspace leads with an action date in the next 180 days.
- **Market Intel**: Highlights actionable signals and opportunities:
  - **Active Buying Agencies**: Agencies buying matching services this month (e.g., Dept of Defense, Veterans Affairs, Agriculture).
  - **Contracts Ending Soon**: Potential re-competes identified within the next 6 months.
  - **New Solicitations**: Solicitations matching your IT, digital, and program support services.
  - **Agency Contacts**: Top displayed contacts for key buying agencies.

## Additional Information

You can manage your account settings, view notifications via **[View All](./notifications.mdx)**, or return to the **[Dashboard](./dashboard.mdx)** at any time using the main navigation.

:::tip
Regularly review your **Lead Prospector** and **Market Intel** sections on the **[Dashboard](./dashboard.mdx)** to stay ahead of expiring contracts and active solicitations.
:::
