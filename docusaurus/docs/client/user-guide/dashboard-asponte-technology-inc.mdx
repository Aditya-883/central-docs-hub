## Overview

The [Dashboard](./dashboard.mdx) serves as the central command hub for Asponte Technology Inc, providing a high-level overview of company readiness, active lead pipelines, and actionable market intelligence. From this page, you can monitor your SAM.gov status, track certifications, evaluate portfolio completion, and review incoming solicitations and agency contacts.

## Screen Reference

![Page Screenshot](/img/client/01-dashboard-asponte-technology-inc-screenshot.png)

## Page Features

- **Organization Profile & Status**: Displays company identity information, including the primary contact, SAM.GOV status, and active certifications (VOSB and SDVOSB).
- **GovCon-Ready Readiness Meters**: Visual progress indicators tracking company profile completion, company portfolio completion, and banner status.
- **Lead Prospector**: Summarizes leads you are actively pursuing, categorizing metrics such as:
  - **SAM.gov**: Solicitations added in the last 14 days.
  - **Ebuy RFx**: GSA eBuy procurement notices added in the last 14 days.
  - **Email Parsers**: Email procurement notices added in the last 14 days.
  - **Symphony RFx**: Symphony procurement notices added in the last 14 days.
  - **New Leads**: Leads added to In Evaluation in the last 7 days.
  - **Active Leads**: Leads currently in Plans to Pursue.
  - **Expiring**: Workspace leads with an action date in the next 180 days.
- **Market Intel**: Highlights actionable signals, matching solicitations, and recompete opportunities, including:
  - **Active Buying Agencies**: Agencies buying matching services.
  - **Contracts Ending Soon**: Potential recompetes within the next 6 months.
  - **New Solicitations**: Solicitations matching IT, digital, and program support services.
  - **Agency Contacts**: Top contacts displayed for key buying agencies.

## Additional Information

You can manage your account settings or review notifications at any time by accessing your [User Profile](./users.mdx), updating your [Change Password](./reset-password.mdx) credentials, or checking [View All](./notifications.mdx) notifications from the top navigation bar.

:::tip
Regularly review your GovCon-Ready portfolio completion meters and expiring workspace leads to ensure all necessary company documentation and active pursuits remain up to date.
:::
