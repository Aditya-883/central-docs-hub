## Overview
The Notifications page allows users to track system alerts, updates, and shared items across their account, such as new shared filters available in GOVCON360. Users can view unread and all notifications, filter their list, and perform individual notification actions. You can navigate back to your [Dashboard](./dashboard.mdx), manage your [User Profile](./users.mdx), or update your [Change Password](./reset-password.mdx) settings using the top navigation and profile menus.

## Page Features
- **Notification Filters**: Toggle between **All** and **Unread** views to manage your items.
- **Notification Cards**: Each notification displays the update type (such as shared filters from team members), timestamps, and details.
- **Notification Actions**: Use the action button on individual notification cards to manage specific alerts.
- **Pagination**: Navigate across multi-page notifications using the page indicators and directional controls like **Next ›**.

## Step-by-Step Workflow
1. Access the notifications area from the top navigation bar.
2. Click on the notification tabs (**All** or **Unread**) to filter the alerts list.
3. Review individual notifications such as shared filters.
4. Click the **Notification actions** button on any notification card to interact with or dismiss the item.
5. Use **Next ›** to view additional notification pages if available.

## Additional Information
The header displays your user initials and a dropdown counter showing the total count of current notifications. You can also quickly access main sections from your navigation menu, including Market Intel, Lead Prospector, Business Development, Teaming Partnerships, Proposals, Contracts, and Administration.

:::tip
Check your unread notifications regularly to stay up to date when colleagues share saved filters or important updates with your GOVCON360 account.
:::
