## Overview
The Notifications page allows you to view and manage system alerts, updates, and shared filter notifications within your GOVCON360 account. Users can review incoming notices regarding shared saved filters, track unread items, and manage individual notifications.

## Screen Reference
![Page Screenshot](/img/client/02-notifications-screenshot.png)

## Page Features
- **Notification Counters**: Displays the total count of notifications and unread items, allowing you to quickly gauge new activity since visiting your [Dashboard](./dashboard.mdx).
- **Notification Filters**: Tab options allowing you to switch between viewing **All** notifications or specifically filter by **Unread** items.
- **Notification Cards**: Individual alert items displaying timestamps, details (such as shared saved filters), and a **Notification actions** button to manage each entry.
- **Pagination Controls**: Navigate across multiple pages of notification history using the pagination footer (e.g., **Page 1 of 3** and **Next ›**).

## Step-by-Step Workflow
1. Access the notifications list by clicking the notification icon displaying your unread count in the top navigation bar.
2. Use the filter tabs at the top of the list to switch between **All** and **Unread** notifications.
3. Review individual notifications, such as new shared filters from team members.
4. Click the **Notification actions** button on any notification card to manage or update its status.
5. Use the pagination controls at the bottom of the page (such as **Next ›**) to view older notifications.
