## Overview

The Dashboard provides a centralized command center for monitoring organizational status, government contracting readiness metrics, lead prospecting activity, and key market signals. Users can review company portfolio completion percentages, check active notices, track pursuit pipelines, and examine relevant awards and solicitations. You can also navigate to **[asponte.com](%7B%7B%27./dashboard-asponte-technology-inc.mdx%27%7D%7D)** or manage your account via **[User Profile](./users.mdx)** and **[Change Password](./reset-password.mdx)**.

## Page Features

- **Company Overview**: Displays organizational identification details, website link to [asponte.com](./dashboard-asponte-technology-inc.mdx), primary contact information, SAM.gov status, and active socioeconomic certifications.
- **GovCon-Ready Status**: Tracks portfolio completion percentages, banner statuses, and steps required to optimize company readiness.
- **Lead Prospector**: Summarizes active pursuits and incoming procurement notices, broken down across several categories:
  - **SAM.gov**: Solicitations added in the last 14 days.
  - **Ebuy RFx**: GSA eBuy procurement notices added in the last 14 days.
  - **Email Parsers**: Email procurement notices added in the last 14 days.
  - **Symphony RFx**: Symphony procurement notices added in the last 14 days.
  - **New Leads**: Leads added to In Evaluation in the last 7 days.
  - **Active Leads**: Leads currently in Plans to Pursue.
  - **Expiring**: Workspace leads with an action date coming up in the next 180 days.
- **Market Intel**: Highlights actionable signals and agency intelligence:
  - **Active Buying Agencies**: Agencies buying matching services this month.
  - **Contracts Ending Soon**: Potential re-competes occurring in the next 6 months.
  - **New Solicitations**: Solicitations matching IT, digital, and program support services.
  - **Agency Contacts**: Top agency contacts for relevant buying departments.
- **Notifications & Profile**: Access system alerts via the notification counter and **[View All](./notifications.mdx)**, or open account settings through **[User Profile](./users.mdx)**.

## Additional Information

Navigation links across the platform allow quick transitions between the Dashboard, Market Intel, Lead Prospector, Business Development, Teaming Partnerships, Proposals, Contracts, and Administration sections.

:::tip
Regularly review your GovCon-Ready portfolio metrics and expiring workspace leads to ensure timely responses to upcoming re-competes and active solicitations.
:::
