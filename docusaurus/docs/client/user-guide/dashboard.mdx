## Overview

The dashboard page serves as the central command center for Asponte Technology Inc, providing a high-level overview of company readiness metrics, lead prospecting status, and actionable market intelligence. Users can review organization details, track active procurements, and navigate quickly across modules like **[User Profile](./users.mdx)** and **[Change Password](./reset-password.mdx)**.

## Page Features

### Company Profile & Readiness
- **Company Overview**: Displays company details including website status (**[asponte.com](./dashboard-asponte-technology-inc.mdx)**), primary contacts, SAM.gov status, and active certifications (VOSB, SDVOSB).
- **GovCon-Ready Meters**: Interactive progress bars tracking readiness milestones such as Company Readiness (90%), Company Portfolio (86%), and Company Banner (100%).

### Lead Prospector
Tracks leads you are actively pursuing across multiple procurement channels:
- **SAM.gov**: SAM.gov solicitations added in the last 14 days.
- **Ebuy RFx**: GSA eBuy procurement notices added in the last 14 days.
- **Email Parsers**: Email procurement notices added in the last 14 days.
- **Symphony RFx**: Symphony procurement notices added in the last 14 days.
- **New Leads**: Leads added to In Evaluation in the last 7 days.
- **Active Leads**: Leads in Plans to Pursue.
- **Expiring**: Workspace leads with an action date in the next 180 days.

### Market Intel
Highlights actionable signals and government contracting opportunities:
- **Active Buying Agencies**: Agencies buying services this month, including Department of Defense, Department of Veterans Affairs, and Department of Agriculture.
- **Contracts Ending Soon**: Potential re-competes identified in the next 6 months.
- **New Solicitations**: Solicitations matching IT, digital, and program support services.
- **Agency Contacts**: Top displayed contacts for active buying agencies like the Department of Defense.

## Additional Information

Users can access notifications using the dropdown menu and manage account settings. To view all recent notifications, select **View All** to navigate to **[View All](./notifications.mdx)**.

:::tip
Regularly review your GovCon-Ready progress bars and active lead counts on the dashboard to ensure all company portfolios and expiring actions remain up to date.
:::
