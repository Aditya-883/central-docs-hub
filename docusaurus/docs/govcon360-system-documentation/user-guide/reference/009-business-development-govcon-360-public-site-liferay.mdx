The GovCon Business Development Tool guides you seamlessly through every stage of pursuing a contract, helping you organize essential agencies and vendors to build a confident strategy. Access the platform by using the navigation options at the top of the page, including **Home**, **Solutions For**, **Features**, **Pricing**, **Resources**, **About**, and **Explore**, or select **Login** or **Schedule a Demo** to get started.

## Header elements

The top navigation bar provides quick access to core information and account actions:

| Element | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Returns you to the main landing page. |
| **Solutions For** | Displays industry solutions and use cases. |
| **Features** | Highlights core product capabilities. |
| **Pricing** | Shows subscription and plan details. |
| **Resources** | Opens guides, documentation, and helpful materials. |
| **About** | Provides company background and information. |
| **Explore** | Lets you discover additional platform tools. |
| **Schedule a Demo** | Opens a scheduling form to book a live walkthrough. |
| **Login** | Takes you to the sign-in page to access your account. |

## Federal Opportunity Pipeline

When you are ready to pursue a contract, it moves to your Opportunities board. This visual layout lets you see where every potential deal stands:
1. Click any opportunity card to open a detailed view containing your notes, documents, and team collaboration tools.
2. Drag and drop the card through the four clear pipeline stages as you complete actions.
