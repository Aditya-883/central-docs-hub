The market intelligence overview page provides insights into federal agency and vendor entities, helping you navigate historical spend and agency structures. You can access this page directly from the public marketing site to learn more about platform capabilities.

## Header elements

Use the top navigation bar to access main sections of the public site, request a demo, or sign in to your account.

| Element | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Home** | Returns you to the main landing page. |
| **Solutions For** | Displays available industry and business solutions. |
| **Features** | Outlines platform capabilities such as market intelligence. |
| **Pricing** | Shows subscription tiers and plans. |
| **Resources** | Provides access to guides, articles, and documentation. |
| **About** | Shares information about the company and team. |
| **Explore** | Opens additional discovery tools and content. |
| **Schedule a Demo** | Opens a form to request a personalized walkthrough. |
| **Login** | Navigates to the sign-in screen. |

## Federal Agency & Vendor Entities

The market intelligence section outlines how historical spend and agency structures turn into a clear roadmap for your business. Use the available page buttons to manage your preferences or explore common questions regarding federal contracting:

- **Cookie Settings**: Adjust your tracking and cookie preferences.
- **Accessibility Settings**: Configure display and navigation options for accessibility.
- **How do I find out which government agencies actually buy my specific product?**
- **Can I see how much the government usually pays for solutions like mine?**
- **How do I know when an existing contract is going to expire so I can prepare to bid?**
- **I am a small business. How can researching other vendors help me?**
- **What are contract vehicles and do I need one to win a bid?**

When the cookie banner appears at the bottom of the screen, you can click **Customize**, **Reject all**, or **Accept all** to manage your consent choices.
